Generic medicine, explained

A generic medicine is not simply an unbranded copy.

Where approved by the relevant regulator, a generic medicine is developed to match a reference brand medicine in its active ingredient, strength, dosage form, route of administration, and intended use. It is evaluated to demonstrate therapeutic equivalence or bioequivalence under the applicable approval standard.

Same therapeutic targetApproved generics are designed to provide the same clinical effect and safety profile as their reference brand medicine.
Different presentationPackaging, colour, shape, name, and certain inactive ingredients may differ.
Professional review mattersNot every product is appropriate for every person or automatically interchangeable in every situation.

Affordable access should still be guided by the prescription, the approved product information, and the advice of a qualified prescriber or pharmacist. The product supplied must match the prescription and applicable requirements.

!Side-effects precautionGeneric medicines, like brand medicines, may cause side effects. If you notice an unexpected, severe, or concerning reaction, contact a qualified healthcare professional promptly; seek urgent local medical care for an emergency.

App support

Installation questions, answered.

Use the correct support path instead of bypassing Android safety controls or sharing your business credentials.

Android says the file may be harmful. What should I do?

Android displays an extra warning for apps installed outside the Play Store. Do not proceed automatically. First confirm that the download link was sent directly by PharmaLink and that the app details match your expected onboarding. Contact your representative if you are unsure.

I cannot find “Install unknown apps.”

Settings vary by device. Search your phone’s Settings for “Install unknown apps.” On some older Android versions, the option may appear under Settings → Security as “Unknown sources.” Enable it only for the trusted app you are using to complete this installation, then turn it off afterwards.

The app will not open after installation.

Restart your phone, confirm that it has enough available storage, and download the file again from the official PharmaLink link. If the issue remains, contact your PharmaLink representative for assistance.

I forgot my User ID, password, or PIN.

Contact your PharmaLink representative. Do not share your credentials through public channels or with anyone who is not an authorised support contact.

Can I install this on iPhone or iPad?

No. This installer is an Android APK file and does not work on iPhone or iPad. Ask PharmaLink whether an iOS version is available.

Frequently asked questions

Questions deserve direct answers.

These answers introduce the service structure. For account-specific information, contact PharmaLink or access the client dashboard.

What does the $15 service fee cover?

It is PharmaLink’s fixed service fee for each shipment request. The medical cost and door-to-door courier charge are separate and are shown as distinct charge categories.

How much can be shipped in one consignment?

The stated shipment limit is not more than 3 kg at a time. This operating limit is intended to reduce supply-chain complications and remains subject to documentation and route review.

Do I need a prescription?

Yes. Prescription-led requests require a valid prescription and supporting information as applicable. PharmaLink does not diagnose, prescribe, or substitute for a licensed clinician.

Can I return a shipment or request a refund?

Requests should be reviewed carefully before confirmation. As stated in the terms, completed shipment requests are generally final, with no return or refund, except where required by applicable law.

Will my package clear customs?

Customs and import decisions are made by the relevant authorities. The recipient is responsible for providing accurate required documentation and meeting applicable import requirements; clearance cannot be guaranteed.

What can I view in the client dashboard?

The dashboard is intended to show available order details, history, shipment tracking, credits, the actual medical bill, and courier charges for your account.
