Frequently asked questions

Questions deserve direct answers.

These answers introduce the service structure. For account-specific information, contact PharmaLink or access the client dashboard.

What does the $15 service fee cover?

It is PharmaLink’s fixed service fee for each shipment request. The medical cost and door-to-door courier charge are separate and are shown as distinct charge categories.

How much can be shipped in one consignment?

The stated shipment limit is not more than 3 kg at a time. This operating limit is intended to reduce supply-chain complications and remains subject to documentation and route review.

Do I need a prescription?

Yes. Prescription-led requests require a valid prescription and supporting information as applicable. PharmaLink does not diagnose, prescribe, or substitute for a licensed clinician.

Can I return a shipment or request a refund?

Requests should be reviewed carefully before confirmation. As stated in the terms, completed shipment requests are generally final, with no return or refund, except where required by applicable law.

Will my package clear customs?

Customs and import decisions are made by the relevant authorities. The recipient is responsible for providing accurate required documentation and meeting applicable import requirements; clearance cannot be guaranteed.

What can I view in the client dashboard?

The dashboard is intended to show available order details, history, shipment tracking, credits, the actual medical bill, and courier charges for your account.

2. Prescription-led requests

Any request involving prescription medicines or medical products must be supported by a valid prescription and accurate information as applicable.   • Provide accurate, complete, and current prescription, order, recipient, and contact information.

PharmaLink does not guarantee customs clearance, delivery time, or delivery outcome. The stated shipment limit is not more than 3 kg at a time, subject to review and applicable requirements.

6. Final orders, returns, and refunds

Clients must recheck prescriptions and orders before confirmation. Because of the nature of supplied items and shipment processing, completed shipment requests are generally final and are not eligible for return or refund, except where required by applicable law or expressly agreed in writing before processing.

Shipping Disclaimer

Last updated: August 19, 2026. This shipping disclaimer is a working website draft and should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before publication.

1. Shipment limit and route

PharmaLink states an operating limit of not more than 3 kg in one shipment to help avoid supply-chain issues and complications. This is not a guarantee that any request will be accepted, shipped, or delivered. Route availability remains subject to review, carrier policies, and applicable requirements.

2. Courier handling

Door-to-door courier handling is coordinated through carrier services. Courier charge is separate from the actual medical cost and the fixed $15 service fee. Carrier collection, transport, handling, delay, loss, damage, or delivery actions are outside PharmaLink’s direct control.

3. Customs, imports, and recipient responsibility

Each shipment may include a client declaration and bill for customs purposes. The recipient is responsible for accurate declarations, requested documentation, import permissions, duties, taxes, and destination-country requirements. Customs authorities and carriers independently decide whether a shipment can proceed, be delayed, be returned, or be disposed of. Clearance is not guaranteed.

4. Timing and tracking

Any estimated time is an estimate only. Tracking information, when available, may be viewed through the client dashboard. Tracking events do not create a guarantee of delivery by a particular date or to a particular recipient.

5. Address accuracy and final delivery

Clients are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address and accessible recipient details. PharmaLink is not responsible for a failed delivery caused by inaccurate details, recipient unavailability, carrier restrictions, customs action, or events outside reasonable control.

6. No promise of import eligibility

This website does not confirm that any product may be imported into any jurisdiction. Clients should obtain independent legal, customs, and medical guidance where needed before proceeding.
